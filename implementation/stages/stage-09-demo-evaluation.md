# Stage 9 — Demo, Alternative Comparison, and Falsification

**Status:** Not started
**Phase:** Evaluation

Read the complete artifact, specification, and recorded QA evidence.

## Objective

Determine whether the artifact is a clear internship demonstration and whether it should be shipped, reduced, or killed.

## Work

1. Run the full synthetic demo without manual repair.
2. Record the exact inputs, outputs, duration, and observed behavior.
3. Repeat the same decision using the simplest spreadsheet or native alternative.
4. Compare clarity, effort, correctness, portability, privacy, and technical demonstration value.
5. Verify that the demo makes no claim about real users, HexCoded performance, model superiority, savings, demand, or product-market fit.
6. Record what a founder can learn from the artifact and what remains Unknown.
7. Apply every Stage 5 success and kill criterion.

## Decision

Record exactly one:

- Ship — package the current artifact.
- Reduce — package a smaller artifact or spreadsheet.
- Kill — document why the premise lost and do not polish further.

Do not conduct external outreach or use private data unless separately authorized. Do not add features to rescue a weak demo.

## Required output

Add the demo evidence, alternative comparison, limitations, and decision to implementation/cumulative-report.md. Update implementation/handoff.md.
