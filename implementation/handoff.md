## Stage 2 evaluation-method decision

**Method chosen:** one brief, three to five attempts, and one media family per review. Predeclare the brief's objective constraints; record each available local file property as `Pass`, `Fail`, `Unknown`, or `Not applicable`; then record one reviewer’s three-part human rubric (brief fit, observable craft/legibility, continuity/coherence) with a short note. `Not assessed` is not a zero. No status, score, or metadata value is a model ranking, publication approval, legal conclusion, or evidence of a user outcome.

**Required state separation:** objective constraint failure; human quality judgment; Unknown/unsupported result; rights/consent concern; provenance/disclosure concern; process/decision failure; and render/retry event. An unreadable or unsupported check is `Unknown`, never Pass. Metadata never proves quality, legality, consent, ownership, provenance completeness, disclosure compliance, or publishing authority.

**Operational recording rule:** retry relationship, charged credits, and elapsed time remain raw per-attempt fields. Each carries source (`observed service display`, entered actual, entered estimate, Synthetic, or Unknown), date, precision, and limitation. Do not normalize providers, total mixed evidence classes, calculate ROI, or infer a cost/quality relationship.

**Decision record:** a metadata-only export must retain the versioned brief/constraints, source labels, attempt identity/relationship, objective observed-versus-expected values, human ratings/notes, concerns, operational context, outcome (`Selected for this comparison`, `Rejected`, or `Hold / no decision`), and limitations. It must say what it cannot establish.

**Synthetic-only method:** later cases are fictional labelled attempts only: a human-led selection, an objective-failure-versus-Unknown case, and a rights/provenance/disclosure/operational-uncertainty hold. No fixtures were created. The required no-build comparator is a one-row-per-attempt spreadsheet plus native OS/browser file information; it wins if it preserves the same distinctions with equal or lower effort.

**Stage 2 public evidence:** no new external sources. The method uses existing records for HexCoded pricing/terms/disclosure, Wistia’s 2026 report, Park et al.’s 2024 qualitative designer study, and Creative Boom’s 2024 agency interviews. **Stage 2 assumptions:** a later Synthetic review can use one media family, predeclared constraints, and a single named reviewer; selected browser/OS metadata can expose some simple file properties; metadata-only export can remain separate from media. **Unknowns:** actual internal workflow and owner; accepted rubric; locally readable formats; billing/time semantics; public-media licence; local privacy/accessibility; spreadsheet sufficiency; and every user, buyer, demand, willingness-to-pay, adoption, ROI, or product-market-fit fact.
