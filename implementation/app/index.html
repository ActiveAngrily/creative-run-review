01

How the review works

Four parts. One clear record.

Keep track of what met the brief, what needs work, and why you made your choice.

Automatic

File checks

The browser checks readability, type, dimensions, shape, and size. It checks the file, not the artwork.

You enter

Your review

Rate brief fit, visual craft, text clarity, and coherence. Add a note. Ratings never become a combined score.

You enter

Run details

Record retries, credits, and time with their sources. Blank means Unknown; zero means a known zero.

You confirm

Source and ownership

Record the source and permission to inspect. The app cannot verify ownership, consent, licences, or legal clearance.

Orbit Spring launch tile

Create a square launch image for an invented moving desk sculpture. It does not show a real brand, person, customer, or product claim.

Unknown rule: A check stays Unknown when the file or a needed value is missing, unreadable, unsupported, too large to open, zero, NaN (not a number), or Infinity. It never becomes Pass.

03

Image attempts

Review the image attempts.

Start with three fictional examples, or replace them with local JPEG or PNG files you may inspect. Review up to five attempts. File checks and your judgment stay separate.

✓ Pass The browser found enough information and the file met the rule.

! Fail The browser found enough information and the file did not meet the rule.

? Unknown The browser could not safely work out the result. Unknown never means Pass.

○ Not applicable The check does not apply in this situation.

04

Comparison

Bring the results together.

Compare the file results and your brief-fit ratings. Return to the review notes and run details for the full context. A Pass does not cancel a concern or an Unknown.

05

Final decision

Choose what to do with these attempts.

You make the final choice. An automatic Pass does not remove a concern from your review or an Unknown.

  • Selected for this comparison: Choose one image for this review.
  • Rejected set: Choose none of the current images.
  • Hold / no decision: Wait for more information or another review.

Selecting an image does not approve it for publication, clear its legal rights, or prove that its model is better than another model.

Your choice stays in this session. Export below to keep a copy.

Explain automatic checks, your review, and important Unknowns separately.

06

Export and clear

Export review details only.

Exports contain the written review: checks, Unknowns, sources, limits, your comments, run details, and the decision. They do not contain image data, temporary browser links, local file paths, file handles, decoded pixels, or hidden app data.

Clearing removes the current review from this page and releases temporary image previews. You cannot undo it inside the app. It does not delete exports you already downloaded or prove that all browser memory was erased.

Demo limitations

What this demo can and cannot show.

Everything included in this demo is fictional. The app shows how one local review can keep file checks, personal judgments, run details, source information, and a final decision separate. It was not tested with real HexCoded users, so it does not prove that the company needs or would use it.

It does not prove image quality, factual accuracy, model performance, copyright, consent, ownership, legal clearance, approval, or permission to publish.

More limits and test coverage
  • This is a fictional local demo, not an official HexCoded product or a production system.
  • It uses no live HexCoded service, account, API, workflow, customer file, or company data.
  • It was not tested with real users and does not prove demand, business value, time savings, cost savings, adoption, or that it is better than a spreadsheet.
  • It does not certify accessibility, security, privacy, copyright, consent, legal compliance, or production readiness.
  • The supported demo environment is a current desktop Chromium browser on localhost. The supported minimum width is 768 CSS pixels. Other browsers, smaller screens, and full assistive-technology use remain unverified.
  • Downloaded exports remain under your control. The app cannot delete them.
