## 11. Validation plan for the provisional lead

H2 is selected only for bounded learning. This remains an **evidence-acquisition and no-build comparison plan**, not authorization for a production product or market claim.

1. **Recruit the Stage 4 sample.** Conduct 8–12 artifact-led interviews with agencies that shipped client-facing short-form video/social work in the last 90 days: three creative/production or creative-ops leads, two account leads, two editors/AI producers, two social publishers, and one to three client brand/legal/rightsholder roles. Include at least two Frame.io/Ziflow users and at least two Canva/Sprout or comparable social-system users.
2. **Trace real releases.** For each participant, inspect one shipped and one delayed/rejected campaign: brief/reference; relevant generation history; proof/version and decision; exact asset and caption; authorized approver/publisher; scheduled/published post; timestamps; actual stack/configuration; and the cheapest process or integration change tried.
3. **Measure consequence.** Record counts or ranges for manual checks, version/caption swaps, rework minutes/hours, chase time, delayed or missed slots, rejected output, duplicate generation, and clearance holds. Do not treat a near-miss as equal to an incident; record it separately.
4. **Test the incumbent first.** Configure the current social system as the release source of truth where possible and apply a locked asset/version identifier, named approval authority, and release check to two comparable releases. This is an operating test, not a prototype.
5. **Verify the economic and trust path.** Identify the primary user, champion, approver, beneficiary, buyer, and budget owner by name/role; obtain participant-approved access using redacted/minimum artifacts; record retention, permission, and deletion constraints.
