## 1. Decision summary and evidence boundary

**Inference:** For the three constructed cases in this record, a configured social-publishing source of truth plus an explicit release rule and H7 operating discipline can cover the same release conditions as a separate Authorized Release Record.

**Inference:** The Authorized Release Record is mechanically testable as a small template, but this exercise does not establish a distinct software premise. It is most useful here as a disposable way to expose which state must be compared.

**Unknown:** Whether any real agency has the described failure, how often it occurs, whether it has a material consequence, whether current tools can be configured as assumed, whether a separate record reduces work, and whether anyone would adopt, approve, buy, retain, or pay for it.

**Decision:** Treat **existing stack plus operating discipline as sufficient for these synthetic cases only**. Keep H2 parked as a narrowed provisional question. Do not advance to a product, prototype, integration, backlog, architecture, roadmap, launch, or commercial test. The strict gate remains 0/13.

## 2. Method and common synthetic controls

Exactly three tabletop cases were constructed. They are not observations, interviews, customer evidence, account tests, or market evidence.

Claim labels in this record mean:

- **Synthetic assumption:** invented input, role, system state, timestamp, identifier, or result used only to exercise the controls.
- **Hypothesis:** behavior a control might produce in a comparable real workflow.
- **Inference:** conclusion supported only by the internal logic of the synthetic comparison.
- **Unknown:** matter that requires permissioned primary evidence or a live configured-system test.

The three responses are:

1. **Synthetic assumption — Authorized Release Record:** a separate one-row or one-card template populated from the proofing and social systems.
2. **Synthetic assumption — configured existing stack:** the current social-publishing queue, named **SOCIAL-SOT** in this exercise, is the sole release source of truth. Its release rule is: a post may progress only when its immutable approved asset/version identifier, exact caption, channel, named decision-maker, publishing authority, scheduled item, and final-post state are complete and mutually consistent; any change returns the item to Hold and requires a new decision.
3. **Synthetic assumption — H7:** one named approver and delegate, a 24-hour decision SLA, a maximum of two revision rounds before re-scoping, reminders at hours 12 and 20, and escalation to an operations owner at hour 24.

**Unknown:** Whether a particular incumbent plan exposes the fields, immutable identifiers, permissions, automation, and blocking rule assumed for SOCIAL-SOT. No vendor capability or live configuration is demonstrated here.

## 7. Smallest useful Authorized Release Record

**Synthetic assumption — experiment format:** one row or card per intended post. It stores links, identifiers, decisions, and exceptions—not assets, credentials, contracts, biometric data, claims evidence, or rights documents.

| Record section | Minimum field |
|---|---|
| Identity and state | Release-record ID; Draft / Hold / Ready / Published / Exception. |
| Creative context | Brief/reference link and creative-output ID. Context does not count as approval. |
| Decision/version state | Proof link; immutable asset ID and exact version; exact caption text and revision; channel; decision; named approver or authorized delegate; decision timestamp. |
| Publishing authorization | Named publishing authority; account; authorized release window; scheduled-post ID/time; final-post link or exception. |
| Rights/consent | Not applicable / Pending / Complete / Hold; owner-controlled evidence link where applicable. |
| Claims substantiation | Not applicable / Pending / Complete / Hold; owner-controlled evidence link where applicable. |
| Provenance | Available source/edit-history reference; never treated as ownership, truth, consent, or approval. |
| Disclosure | Required / Not required / Pending; policy owner; channel-specific mechanism where applicable. |

**Synthetic assumption — release rule:** Ready is allowed only when required fields are complete and the scheduled asset/version, exact caption, channel, account, and authority match the approved state. Any material change returns the record to Hold and requires a new decision.

**Unknown:** The acceptable number of fields, completion burden, ownership, terminology, and usefulness in a real workflow.

## 8. H2 disconfirmation conditions

H2 should close rather than advance when any of the following is demonstrated on permissioned real work:

- **Hypothesis:** a configured social source of truth reliably binds the exact approved asset/version, exact caption, channel, decision, publisher authority, scheduled item, and final post with equal or lower effort;
- **Hypothesis:** a naming, import, attachment-lock, approval, or release rule prevents the mismatch without material residual cost;
- **Hypothesis:** H7 shows that the apparent H2 symptom was decision ownership or delay rather than release-state continuity;
- **Hypothesis:** the Authorized Release Record duplicates state, becomes stale, is bypassed, or increases reconciliation;
- **Hypothesis:** exact-state failures are isolated, immaterial, unsafe to inspect, or not testable without a complete product; or
- **Unknown:** no real recurrence, consequence, reachable role, safe access path, incumbent insufficiency, or economic path can be established.

## 9. H7 and existing-stack stop conditions

- **Inference:** Stop a software premise for decision delay when a named approver/delegate, 24-hour SLA, revision cap, reminders, and escalation produce a timely decision on comparable real work.
- **Inference:** Stop the separate-record premise when the named social source of truth exposes immutable release identifiers, blocks incomplete or changed state, and retains the scheduled/final trace with equal or lower effort.
- **Inference:** Stop both premises when the combined operating rule and current configuration handle decision timing and release-state continuity without a material residual failure.
- **Unknown:** Whether these conditions hold in any real agency, tool plan, or account.

## 10. Effect on the reversible H2 research bet
